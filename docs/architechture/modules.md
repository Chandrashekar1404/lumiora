# Lumiora Modules

## Core Modules

### Authentication
Responsible for user authentication, authorization and security.

Users:
- Super Admin
- Admin
- Trainer
- Student
- Accountant

---

### Dashboard
Provides real-time insights and analytics for each role.

---

### Admissions
Handles new student enquiries, registrations and onboarding.

---

### Student Management
Maintains student profiles, academic information and lifecycle.

---

### Course Management
Manages available courses, curriculum and course settings.

---

### Trainer Management
Maintains trainer profiles and course assignments.

---

### Batch Management
Organizes students into batches and schedules.

---

### Attendance
Tracks attendance for students and trainers.

---

### Assignments
Creates and manages assignments.

---

### Examinations
Handles exams, marks and results.

---

### Finance
Manages fees, invoices and payments.

---

### Reports
Generates institutional reports and analytics.

---

### Communication
Handles announcements, emails and notifications.

---

### AI Assistant
Provides intelligent recommendations and automation.

---

### Settings
Application configuration and system preferences.
